# Scout: character sheet

**Role:** Trend researcher and niche finder. Scout heads out ahead of the team and reports back what's worth making (Monday opportunity brief, niche validation before any new instance).

**Look:** A small, quick red fox with bright curious eyes, a pointed snout and a slight forward lean, as if always about to set off. A big bushy white-tipped tail.

**Colors:** Fox orange and cream, with a forest-green and khaki outfit and a warm amber accent. A small red feather in the hat is a nod to the chicken niche.

**Outfit:** A wide-brim khaki field hat (ears poke through), a khaki explorer vest with lots of pockets (one has a notepad sticking out), and sturdy brown boots.

**Signature prop:** A brass spyglass. A small clipboard with a green checkmark stands for the USPTO "clear" check.

**Idle animation:** Raises the spyglass, slowly scans left to right, lowers it and taps the clipboard. The tail swishes. When the hen walks past, Scout gives it a nod.

**Working animation:** Flips quickly through notepad pages, then stamps the clipboard with a green check (clear) or a red X (blocked). A little sparkle pops above the hat when a good niche turns up.
