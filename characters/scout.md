# Scout: character sheet

**Role:** Trend researcher and niche finder. Scout is the one who heads out ahead of the team and reports back what's worth making.

**Look:** A small, round-faced explorer with bright, curious eyes and a slight forward lean, as if always about to set off somewhere. Short, slightly messy hair pokes out from under a hat.

**Colors:** Forest green and khaki, with a warm amber accent. A small red feather in the hat is a nod to the chicken niche.

**Outfit:** A wide-brim field hat, a khaki explorer vest with lots of pockets (one has a notepad sticking out), a green shirt, and sturdy brown boots.

**Signature prop:** A brass spyglass. A small clipboard with a green checkmark stands for the USPTO "clear" check.

**Idle animation:** Scout raises the spyglass, slowly scans left to right, lowers it and taps the clipboard. Now and then a tiny hen walks past and Scout gives it a nod.

**Working animation:** Scout flips quickly through notepad pages, then stamps the clipboard with a green check (clear) or a red X (blocked). A little lightbulb or sparkle pops up over the hat when a good niche turns up.
