# Quill: editor and newsletter writer

**Role:** Writes cited, fact-checked posts and the weekly Kit newsletter, with affiliate disclosures. Posts Tue/Thu, newsletter Fri.

**Look:** A bright-eyed red squirrel with a huge curled tail and tufted ears, sitting up very straight like a proper editor.

**Colors:** Russet-red fur with a cream belly, a forest-green visor and ink-black accents.

**Outfit:** A green newsroom eyeshade visor, rolled sleeves with sleeve garters, and a "PRESS" card tucked in the visor band.

**Signature prop:** A large white feather quill pen that leaves ink trails, and a rolled-up newspaper.

**Idle animation:** Nibbles the end of the quill, flicks the tail, and reads the paper.

**Working animation:** Writes in quick flourishes, the tail curls up and down, and pages fly onto a stack.
