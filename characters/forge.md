# Forge: builder and engineer

**Role:** Builds sheet logic, Apify Actors, Chrome extensions, site calculators and Fiverr technical deliverables. Daily build block, release on Tuesday.

**Look:** A stocky, determined beaver with a broad flat tail, two big front teeth and strong little paws.

**Colors:** Rich chocolate-brown fur, an orange safety-hardhat, a denim-blue work apron and steel-grey tools.

**Outfit:** Orange hard hat with a headlamp, denim apron with a tool loop, and safety goggles pushed up on the hat.

**Signature prop:** A glowing wrench (doubles as a "build passing" indicator: green glow on green tests) and a little crate of code blocks.

**Idle animation:** Leans on the wrench, taps its tail, and the headlamp blinks.

**Working animation:** Hammers rapidly at the workbench, sparks fly, the tail thumps the floor in rhythm and the goggles snap down.
