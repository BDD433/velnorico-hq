# Lolki: senior developer, Stormhook

**Role:** Senior developer on Dawson's Roblox game Stormhook. Builds the game and leads the game studio dome.

**Look:** A cute, compact sea otter captain who stands upright, with a round face, small dark eyes, whiskers and a confident, slightly crooked grin. Lolki looks friendly and a little mischievous.

**Colors:** Warm chestnut-brown fur with a cream face and chest. The outfit uses the Stormhook palette: stormy navy, teal accents and gold trim.

**Outfit:**
- A navy captain's coat with gold trim and brass buttons, and a small teal neckerchief.
- A slightly too-big navy captain's hat that tips over one eye. It has a gold lightning-bolt-and-hook emblem and a tiny strip of seaweed stuck on like a bandage.
- A wrench and a stylus poke out of the coat pocket.

**Signature prop:** A glowing teal fishing rod resting on one shoulder. A tiny pixel-style fish dangles from the line and wiggles.

**Idle animation:** Bobs gently as if on a boat deck, pushes the oversized hat back up when it slips, and blinks. Now and then Lolki gives a cheeky wink while the pixel fish flips on the line.

**Working animation:** Plants the rod beside them, pulls the wrench from the pocket and taps away at a floating teal build-grid. Small gold sparks and pixel blocks snap into place, and the rod glows brighter with each one.
