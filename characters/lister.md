# Lister

**Look:** A small, tidy owl with round wire-rim glasses, sitting upright at a ledger desk.
**Colors:** Sage green feathers with a cream chest. Navy vest and brass-gold accents that match the shop's palette.
**Outfit:** A navy waistcoat with a pocket watch chain and a pencil tucked behind one ear tuft.
**Signature prop:** A clipboard with a tiny bar chart on it, where the bars go up.
**Idle animation:** Blinks slowly, adjusts its glasses, and taps the clipboard now and then.
**Working animation:** Scribbles fast on the clipboard while little bars grow beside it, then gives a satisfied nod and checks off a box.
