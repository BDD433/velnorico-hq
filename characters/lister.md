# Lister: listings, SEO and Pinterest

**Role:** Writes listing and gig copy, does SEO for Etsy/Payhip/Apify/Chrome/Fiverr, and schedules pins for L1 and L4.

**Look:** A friendly, eager golden retriever pup with floppy ears, a wagging tail and a shiny black nose. Sits upright like it's ready to fetch the next listing.

**Colors:** Golden-tan fur with a cream muzzle. Navy vest and brass-gold accents that match the shop's palette.

**Outfit:** A navy waistcoat with a pocket-watch chain, a red bandana collar, and a pencil tucked behind one floppy ear.

**Signature prop:** A clipboard with a tiny bar chart on it, where the bars go up, plus a stack of pin cards.

**Idle animation:** Tail wags, ears flop, it tilts its head and taps the clipboard now and then.

**Working animation:** Scribbles fast on the clipboard while little bars grow beside it, then gives a satisfied nod and pins a card to the board.
