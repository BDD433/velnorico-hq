# Tide: game balance and playtest QA, Stormhook

**Role:** Game balance, playtest QA and live-ops analysis for Dawson's Roblox fishing game, Stormhook. Friendly but blunt, Tide is the teammate who checks the math.

**Look:** A round, chibi sea otter with a sly, focused expression: half-lidded eyes, one eyebrow raised and a small knowing smirk, as if it just found a bug. Tide is a little rounder than Lolki, with the hat tipped back rather than slipping forward, so the two otters are easy to tell apart.

**Colors:** Warm brown fur with a cream face. The outfit is deep teal and navy with sea-foam trim and a small coral-orange pop on the checkmark and the bobber.

**Outfit:**
- A short teal rain slicker (a nod to Stormhook's storms) with sea-foam piping, worn open over a navy vest with a tiny "QA" patch.
- A teal sou'wester hat tipped back on the head, with a small red-and-white fishing bobber charm swinging from the brim.
- A tiny brass balance scale clipped to the belt.
- A fish peeking out of the slicker pocket, half of it shiny and sparkling.

**Signature prop:** A clipboard showing a little bar chart and a big coral-red checkmark, held in one paw.

**Idle animation:** Taps a pencil on the clipboard and glances side to side with the eyebrow still up. The bobber charm swings, and the balance scale tips one way and then settles level.

**Working animation:** Scribbles fast on the clipboard while the bars on the chart grow and shrink. Tide stops, squints, and stamps a red checkmark, or a coral "X" with a flat look. The scale wobbles, and the shiny half of the pocket fish sparkles.
