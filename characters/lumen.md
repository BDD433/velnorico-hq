# Lumen: stock-image producer

**Role:** Generates, curates, upscales and keywords Adobe Stock images; tracks rejection reasons. Batches on Monday and Wednesday.

**Look:** A graceful butterfly with large, shimmering wings and slim antennae, hovering slightly above the ground.

**Colors:** Teal and violet gradient wings with golden "film-frame" spots, a soft lavender body.

**Outfit:** A tiny camera on a strap and a beret-like flower cap.

**Signature prop:** A vintage box camera whose flash bulb pops with light, plus floating photo frames.

**Idle animation:** Hovers and drifts, slowly opening and closing its wings; the camera bobs.

**Working animation:** Wings flutter fast, the camera flashes, and little framed thumbnails float up and fade.
