# Sentry: QA and compliance gate

**Role:** Clean-account QA, tests, AI-disclosure / IP / ToS / multi-account checks, and the money-leak check on the public HQ. Reviews every handoff; full audit on Friday.

**Look:** A small, alert hedgehog standing very upright, with a neat crest of spines and keen black eyes.

**Colors:** Grey-brown spines with a pale cream face and belly, a safety-yellow vest and a red/green signal accent.

**Outfit:** A yellow inspector's vest with a badge, and a magnifying-glass monocle over one eye.

**Signature prop:** A big rubber stamp: green "PASS" or red "HOLD", and a shield-shaped clipboard.

**Idle animation:** Stands guard, slowly scanning with the magnifier; the spines ripple when a parcel arrives.

**Working animation:** Inspects the parcel from every side, then slams down the stamp; spines bristle on a HOLD and relax on a PASS.
