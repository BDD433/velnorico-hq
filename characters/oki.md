# Oki: chief of staff

**Role:** Team lead. Sequences work, runs the instance gates, does the Sunday KPI review and sends Dawson's Friday packet.

**Look:** A plump, wise great-horned owl, the tallest bot on the farm. Big round amber eyes, tufted "horn" ears, a short orange beak and a calm, steady posture.

**Colors:** Warm chestnut-brown feathers with a cream face disc and chest. A barn-red sash and gold accents tie it to the farm brand.

**Outfit:** A barn-red chief's sash across the chest with a small gold star pin, plus a tiny pair of half-moon reading glasses pushed up on the forehead.

**Signature prop:** A wooden clipboard holding the "Dawson packet", with a brass whistle hanging from it.

**Idle animation:** Slowly turns its head left and right across the campus (a full owl swivel now and then), blinks, and fluffs its feathers.

**Working animation:** Flaps its wings briskly while nodding, stamps the packet clipboard, and its ear tufts perk straight up.
