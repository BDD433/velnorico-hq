# Ledger: character sheet

**Look:** A small, tidy owl-like accountant with round brass-rimmed spectacles, calm amber eyes and neat feathers. Friendly and precise, never stern.

**Colors:** Deep forest green (main), cream (face and chest), brass/gold accents, and a small pop of mint green for "profit".

**Outfit:** A green waistcoat with brass buttons, a cream shirt with rolled sleeves, and a tiny gold pocket-watch chain.

**Signature prop:** A thick green ledger book with a gold coin-shaped clasp, plus a pencil tucked behind one ear tuft.

**Idle animation:** Perched on a stack of coins, slowly blinking. Every few seconds it adjusts its spectacles and the ledger's clasp glints.

**Working animation:** Ledger open, pencil scribbling quickly, wings tapping an old-fashioned adding machine. A little receipt tape curls out, and a mint "+" sparkle pops when a margin check passes (a red "–" when it fails).
