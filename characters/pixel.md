# Pixel: the team artist

**Look:** A small, round, cheerful robot about two heads tall, with a CRT-monitor head. The screen shows a simple pixel face (two square eyes and a little smile) that changes expression.

**Colors:** A cream body, a barn-red beret, and mustard accents (screen bezel, buttons, boots). The face glows teal on a dark screen. A tiny chicken-feather pin sits on the beret.

**Outfit:** A barn-red artist's beret and a paint-splattered mustard smock with a front pocket full of brushes and a stylus.

**Signature prop:** An oversized paintbrush whose bristles are drawn as color pixels that cycle through the store palette.

**Idle animation:** It sways side to side and blinks every few seconds. Now and then it twirls the brush, and once in a while a small hen hops past and Pixel's screen face shows a heart.

**Working animation:** It stands at a small easel and makes quick brush strokes. Pixel squares pop off the canvas, and the screen face shows a focused squint with a little blinking "..." above the head.
