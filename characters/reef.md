# Reef: art, audio and asset producer, Stormhook

**Role:** Makes the art, audio and assets for Dawson's Roblox ocean game, Stormhook.

**Look:** A small, round, chibi octopus with eight curly arms. Reef has big friendly dark eyes with white highlights, rosy cheeks and a cheerful little smile. It's simple and polished, so it reads clearly at small sizes.

**Colors:** A teal body that fades to aqua at the arm tips, with a few pale spots on the head. The suckers and cheeks are soft coral pink. Accents are a navy cap, gold trim and sunny-yellow headphones.

**Outfit:** A tiny navy captain's cap with a gold fishhook badge (Stormhook) perched on top, and big over-ear headphones in sunny yellow.

**Signature props (one per arm):**
- a paintbrush dripping teal paint
- a stylus
- a little fishing rod with a glowing lure
- a seashell microphone
- a cute cartoon puffer-fish model held up proudly like a sculpture

The spare arms wave or keep the beat.

**Idle animation:** Floats and bobs gently. The arms sway out of sync, the head nods to the music in the headphones, and now and then a small bubble pops above the cap.

**Working animation:** All eight arms go at once. One paints a teal stroke, one taps the stylus, one sings into the shell mic as tiny music notes float up, and one turns the puffer model to inspect it. Then the puffer inflates for a beat and Reef beams.
